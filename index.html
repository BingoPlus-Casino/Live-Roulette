01 / Fundamentals

How does roulette work?

Roulette is a wheel-based table game in which players select outcomes before a ball lands in a numbered pocket. The layout includes individual numbers, red and black selections, odd/even bets, and number groups. The exact betting options and rules depend on the table.

Roulette wheel

The physical wheel determines the result. A European roulette wheel has 37 pockets (0–36); an American wheel has 38 (0, 00, and 1–36).

Online roulette

Digital roulette can use a software-generated result or a live-streamed physical wheel. Read the game rules to understand which format is offered.

Live roulette

Live tables typically stream a dealer and wheel in real time, while players use an on-screen interface to place bets within a defined window.

03 / The mathematics

Understanding the house edge

On a standard European single-zero roulette wheel, the house edge on ordinary bets is approximately 2.70%. On a standard American double-zero wheel, it is approximately 5.26%. Some special rule variants differ. These are theoretical long-run figures, not predictions of an individual session.

Neither patterns in previous spins nor popular betting systems can reliably predict the next independent result. Treat roulette as entertainment with financial risk, not as an income strategy.

04 / Search terminology

What does “dirty roulette” mean?

Dirty roulette is not a standardized name for a recognized roulette wheel format. The phrase can appear in informal searches, unrelated contexts, or as a custom game title. If you encounter it on a gaming website, verify the actual rules, wheel configuration, and operator information rather than assuming it represents a distinct roulette variant.

Similarly, searches for roulette wheel online may refer to entertainment wheels, educational simulators, or real-money casino games. Those are different products with different risks and legal requirements.

05 / Common questions

Roulette FAQ

What is live roulette?

Live roulette uses a real roulette wheel and dealer streamed through a digital interface. Rules and table limits vary by provider.

How many numbers are on a roulette wheel?

European roulette has 37 pockets, including a single zero. American roulette has 38 pockets, including zero and double zero.

Is roulette wheel online the same as live roulette?

Not always. Online roulette may be software-based, live-streamed, or simply a free educational wheel simulator.

Is dirty roulette an official game type?

No standard roulette ruleset is universally known by that term. Check the title and published rules of any specific product using the phrase.

Can a roulette strategy guarantee winnings?

No. A betting pattern does not remove the house edge or guarantee a favorable result.
